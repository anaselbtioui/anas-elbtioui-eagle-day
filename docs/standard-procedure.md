# Motor accident: first-principles chain

Morocco. Private motor insurance. This is process guidance from official ACAPS material, not a legal opinion and not a coverage decision.

The product must not determine liability, guarantee, or payout. Humans and the insurer do that.

---

## Actors

| Actor | Role |
|---|---|
| **Motorist** | Person in the crash. Must stay safe, capture evidence, then notify the insurer. |
| **Other driver / third party** | Needed for a joint report when they can be identified and they cooperate. |
| **Police / gendarmerie** | State. Write a **procès-verbal (PV)** when the situation requires an official record. |
| **Assisteur** | Contractual roadside help (tow, sometimes help writing the constat), **if** that guarantee exists. Not the insurer’s claims decision. |
| **Broker (courtier)** | Intermediary. Often holds the contract and the file, and carries the déclaration to the insurer. Does not pay. Does not decide fault. |
| **Insurer (assureur)** | Opens the official file once notified. Selects which guarantees may apply, assesses responsibility, appoints the expert, pays or refuses. |
| **Expert** | Evaluates damage. That evaluation is not a promise of indemnity. |
| **FGAC** | Fund that may intervene for **bodily** harm when the responsible vehicle is unknown or uninsured. Not a consumer form in this product. |

Broker ≠ insurer ≠ assistance ≠ police.

---

## What is not a claim

These happen **before** insurance has a file:

- Calling emergency services or police
- Filling a **constat à l’amiable** (joint report between drivers)
- Obtaining a **PV**
- Taking photos, exchanging identities, checking permis and attestation
- Calling assistance for tow or on-the-spot help

Those are **evidence and safety**. They do not open an insurance file by themselves.

---

## The chain

### 1. Now — safety

If someone is injured or in danger: alert the competent authorities first. Do not collect insurance data yet.

An accident with **bodily harm** requires a **PV**.

### 2. Now — evidence

**Material damage, other driver identified and willing:** stay on scene, complete the **constat à l’amiable** (names, addresses, licence numbers, plates, date, time). Photograph the place and the damage. Check that documents (permis, attestation) are valid.

**Other driver refuses, flees, or is unknown:** do not treat a half-constat as enough. Call police / gendarmerie for a **PV**. The PV is what later proves the event, including that the other party is unknown.

**Assistance:** if the contract includes it, the assisteur may help write the constat and/or move the vehicle. That is a service under the contract, running **beside** the claim, not instead of it.

Output of this phase: **constat or PV**, photos, identities. Still no official claim.

### 3. Later — official claim starts

The insurance claim starts when the motorist **notifies their insurer** (*déclaration de sinistre*) and transmits the constat **or** PV plus other useful pieces.

This notification often goes **through the broker**, who already has the policy. The legal counterpart of the file is still the **insurer**.

ACAPS material-damage guidance: inform the insurer quickly, and in their example **within 5 days** of the accident. Missing that delay can put indemnity at risk. Treat the five days as **published guidance**, not as a deadline the interface calculates or enforces as law.

Without a constat or PV, the déclaration is incomplete. The next step is to obtain that document, not to pretend the file is ready.

### 4. After the déclaration — insurer’s file

Once informed, the insurer:

- considers which **guarantees** may be activated
- considers **share of responsibility**
- may appoint an **expert**

The expert’s preliminary view is **not** an undertaking to pay.

Indemnity, when it happens, may run:

- through the other party’s **RC**, or
- through **CID** (the motorist’s own insurer pays, then settles with the other company), or
- through **contractual** guarantees (e.g. collision, tierce), according to the contract.

Repair: if the motorist uses their own garage they typically pay then seek indemnity; a **garagiste conventionné** may be paid by the insurer. The garage estimate should be validated by the expert before work.

Disagreement with the insurer is a **recours** procedure, not a second “submit claim” action.

The product stops at: file received, missing pieces listed, next **human** step named. It does not announce who is at fault or what will be paid.

## Parallel tracks (do not fold into the material claim)

| Situation | Extra path |
|---|---|
| Injury | PV + bodily indemnity rules (dahir of 1984). Not the same as a bumper file. |
| Unknown / uninsured vehicle + bodily harm | Possible **FGAC**, often after a court decision or a transaction. |
| Accident on the way to work | Also declare to the **employer** (work-accident). Employer has its own delay toward its insurer. |
| Assistance (immobilised vehicle) | Call the assistance number from the **contract**, distinct from a general customer-service number. |

---

## Three product entries (derived from the chain)

Same motorist, two moments. Broker is a different job.

1. **Motorist now** — safety and evidence. Branch: injury / unknown driver → authorities and PV; otherwise constat, photos, assistance if covered. No déclaration form.
2. **Motorist later** — déclaration de sinistre: what happened, what documents exist, what is missing, send to insurer via broker, then tracking that does not imply payment.
3. **Broker** — find the person, the policy, the pieces, the gaps, the next call. Hand the file to the insurer. Never speak as if the broker had decided coverage.

A contact directory is not a fourth journey. It is labelled references: emergency authorities vs 24/7 assistance vs general insurer line vs broker.

---

## Hinge

```
NOW (evidence)  →  constat or PV + photos
                    ↓
LATER           →  déclaration to insurer (often via broker)  = official claim starts
                    ↓
INSURER         →  guarantees, responsibility, expert, pay or refuse
```

**Now becomes a claim only at the déclaration.** The quality of that claim depends on what NOW captured.
